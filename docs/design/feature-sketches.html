Settled · rates & compounding

Small rate, every day, fully adjustable

Parents choose how often interest compounds (daily, weekly or monthly) and set a rate floor and ceiling for that period. Daily tasks move the kid between them. The default is daily at 0.10%–0.20% a day. That's low enough to be believable and still shows up as a few cents every day on a small balance.

Whatever the cadence, interest is worked out every day, so the kid can always see what their money made today. On weekly or monthly cadence, it builds up and lands in the balance on payday or at the end of the month. Fractions of a cent carry over, so tiny balances still grow.

Maya's garden

Example kid: Maya started with $20 six weeks ago, gets $5 every Saturday, and spent $8 on stickers in week 3. Her rate is 0.16% a day (3 of 5 daily tasks done), and a parent boosted her on Monday for a great report card.

Task rewards · try it

Check it off, see it pay

Each task shows its reward before the kid taps it: a rate bump for today (sun) or cash (leaf). Finishing a set like Morning routine pays a bonus, and finishing every daily task is a Perfect Day. The celebration gets bigger with what the reward is worth to this kid. Tap the tasks on the left.

Parent screens · add, subtract, set the rules

The parent side

Parents handle every add and subtract and all the settings. Interest and allowance post on payday automatically, so the manual work is mostly logging spending and approving tasks.

iPhone + iPad · linking a kid's device

Maya gets her own iPad

Parents sign in on their phone with Apple. A kid's iPad never gets a password: a parent shows a one-time code, the iPad scans it, and it stays signed in as that kid until a parent removes it.

1 · Parent taps "Link Maya's iPad"Their phone shows a QR code and an 8-character code. It works once and expires in 10 minutes.
2 · Maya opens the appOn an iPad, "I'm a kid" comes first. She scans the QR, or types the code if the camera is off.
3 · LinkedThe parent's screen flips to "Linked!" on its own. The iPad gets a kid-only sign-in: Maya can check tasks and explore, but can't change money or rules.
4 · Parents stay in controlDevices lists every linked phone and iPad. Removing one signs it out immediately. Siblings can share one iPad with a switcher.

Story · The two gardeners

Sam and Jo each found a magic seed that grows one new seed every week. Sam ate his right away. Jo planted hers, and every seed it made got planted too. By summer Jo had a whole garden and Sam had a snack. Your GrowBucks work the same way, and interest is the new seeds.
